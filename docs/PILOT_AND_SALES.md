# SheetSort Pilot and Sales Pack

## Pilot plan

Run a 60-90 day pilot in the Exam Cell or Placement Cell. Baseline report time and duplicate errors in week 1; review usage, failures, and feedback weekly; measure time saved, accuracy, unassisted completion, and adoption at close.

Weekly check-in: reports processed; median preparation time; errors/blocked tasks; requested Recipes; privacy incidents; next-week owner.

Feedback questions: What report did you prepare? Did SheetSort reduce steps? What result was unclear? Did you trust the duplicate/date decisions? What should be added or removed?

Final report template: baseline; pilot cohort; usage; time/error metrics; staff feedback; incidents; recommendation; approved next actions.

## One-page sales message

**Problem:** staff repeatedly clean and split inconsistent spreadsheets by hand.  
**Solution:** SheetSort turns an uploaded file plus a saved Recipe into a standardized downloadable report.  
**Benefits:** faster reports, fewer duplicate errors, repeatable departmental formats, local processing, and audit visibility.  
**Offer:** free 60-day pilot for one department, including setup, training, support, and a closing report.

## Demo script

Upload a messy student file; preview it; trim spaces and standardize headers; sort by department and roll number; show duplicate review; filter/split; save the Recipe; apply it to a second file; export. Close with the actual counts and elapsed time, for example: “42 duplicates reviewed and a department report generated in 12 seconds.”

## Pricing and objections

Department licence: SGD 1,000-3,000/year. College-wide: SGD 5,000-15,000/year. Paid plans include updates, training, support, and maintenance.

- “Excel sorts already.” SheetSort makes repeatable multi-step workflows safer and shareable.
- “Is data safe?” It is designed for self-hosted, in-memory processing; the college controls access and retention.
- “ERP integration?” Not in the MVP; use export/import first and scope integration after the pilot.
- “Who maintains it?” The paid agreement defines release, support, backup, and handover responsibilities.

Proposal email: “Could we schedule a 30-minute demonstration of SheetSort? It helps administrative teams turn inconsistent Excel files into standardized reports faster, while reducing duplicate and manual-formatting errors. We can provide a no-cost 60-day pilot for one department.”
