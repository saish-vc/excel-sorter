# SheetSort - Pricing Sheet

*Pricing for college deployment (post-pilot)*

## 💰 Pricing Model

### 1. Free 60-Day Pilot
- **Cost**: Free
- **Duration**: 60 days
- **Includes**: Full access to all features
- **Department scope**: One department
- **Support**: Email support during pilot
- **End date**: Automatic expiry after 60 days
- **Transition**: Eligible for department license purchase

### 2. Department License
- **Cost**: SGD 1,000–3,000 per year
- **Duration**: 12 months (renewable)
- **Includes**:
  - Full software access for entire department
  - Unlimited file processing
  - Recipe sharing within department
  - Email support
  - Software updates and point releases
  - Access to audit log
  - Backup and restore procedures
- **Additional users**: Included (entire department)
- **Renewal rate**: SGD 800-2,500 per year (after first year)

### 3. College-Wide License
- **Cost**: SGD 5,000–15,000 per year
- **Duration**: 12 months (renewable)
- **Includes**:
  - Unlimited department access (all administrative departments)
  - Centralized recipe database
  - User accounts with role-based access
  - Priority email/phone support
  - Software updates and major releases
  - Annual training webinar for staff
  - Audit log review and reporting
  - Backup and disaster recovery support
- **Additional support**: Available at SGD 500/hour
- **Renewal rate**: SGD 4,000-12,000 per year (after first year)

### 4. Add-on Options

| Add-on | Cost | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **Additional Support Block** | SGD 500/hour | Priority support beyond included level |
| **Training Webinar** | SGD 800/session | Live training for up to 20 staff |
| **Custom Integration** | Quote-based | ERP/SIS integration, custom workflows |
| **Extended Warranty** | 15% of license | Additional 6 months support |
| **On-Premises Hosting** | Quote-based | College hosts the application internally |

## 💵 Payment Terms

### Department License
- **Upfront payment**: Annual fee due at start of year
- **or**: Monthly installments (SGD 85-250/month, extra 5% fee)
- **Payment methods**: Bank transfer, credit card, institutional purchase order
- **Invoice**: Provided upon payment, GST/Tax ID required for institutions

### College-Wide License
- **Upfront payment**: Annual fee due at start of year
- **or**: Two installments (50% at start, 50% at 6-month mark, extra 8% fee)
- **Payment methods**: Bank transfer, institutional purchase order, letter of credit
- **Invoice**: Provided upon payment, detailed breakdown included
- **Tax**: GST applicable as per college tax status

### Pilot-to-Purchase Transition
- **Pilot cost**: Free (60 days)
- **If purchasing Department License**: Pilot fee **waived** (included in license)
- **If purchasing College-Wide License**: Pilot fee **credited** toward first year (up to SGD 500 value)
- **If not purchasing**: Pilot simply expires after 60 days, no obligation

## 📞 Sales Information

### Sales Cycle
1. **Initial contact**: Department head or administrative officer
2. **Demo**: 5-minute live demo (provided)
3. **Pilot offer**: 60-day free pilot
4. **Evaluation**: Department uses SheetSort, collects feedback
5. **Decision**: Purchase license or end pilot
6. **Onboarding**: Setup, training, migration (if applicable)

### Sales Cycle Timeline
| Stage | Duration | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Initial contact | 1-2 weeks | Email/phone outreach |
| Demo scheduling | 1 week | 5-minute live demo |
| Pilot setup | 1 week | Install, train staff |
| Pilot execution | 60 days | Free usage, feedback collection |
| Decision point | At day 60 | Purchase or end |
| Onboarding | 1-2 weeks | Setup, training |
| **Total** | **~3 months** | From initial contact to operational |

### Key Decision Makers
- **Department Head**: Approves pilot participation
- **IT Administrator**: Evaluates technical requirements, installation
- **Administrative Staff**: Actual users, provide feedback
- **College Administration**: Final purchase decision (for college-wide licenses)

### Objection-Handling Guidelines

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Excel already has sorting/filtering" | "Excel is great for basic tasks, but SheetSort automates the full workflow (cleaning + sorting + filtering + deduplication + export) in minutes vs. hours. Plus, we save your recipes for reuse." |
| "Is student data safe?" | "All processing happens locally on your college servers. No data leaves the premises. We never modify your original files. Audit logs track actions without storing PII." |
| "Can this integrate with our ERP?" | "Current version processes local Excel/CSV files. ERP integration available as custom add-on (additional cost). College-wide license includes priority development consideration." |
| "Who maintains it?" | "We provide software updates and support. Department license includes email support; college-wide includes email + phone. We also provide documentation and training webinars." |
| "What happens if we leave college?" | "Your recipes and data remain in your SQLite database. You can continue using the locally-installed version. Data export is always available." |
| "Can we try before buying?" | **"Yes - 60-day free pilot with full features."** |
